# Control Center Deskmat Art Design

## Design read

TechChancellor is a personal technical-intelligence control center. The interface should feel like a private illustrated intelligence studio rather than a conventional administration dashboard.

## Approved visual direction

- Build the home screen around one complete panoramic illustration.
- Use an original adult female adviser with a warm, relaxed, slightly sensual presence.
- Place the adviser on the right and let the environment fade into quiet negative space on the left for operational content.
- Combine a softly lit studio, pinned research papers, technical notes, and restrained blue-grey and dusty-rose accents.
- Keep the composition continuous. Do not scatter particles, independent ornaments, floating mini-cards, or neon graffiti across the scene.
- Use a thin translucent reading layer over the left-side negative space instead of an opaque dashboard card.
- Preserve the five-panel capability gallery, but make it visually secondary and use one coherent illustration system.

## Reference synthesis

- References 2 and 4: integrated character and environment, relaxed pose, soft light.
- References 8 and 10: research-paper wall and intelligence-work context.
- Reference 14: concentrated subject plus generous dark negative space.
- References 5 and 12: edge framing that keeps the center quiet.

The final artwork must be original. It may reuse composition principles, palette relationships, and visual rhythm, but must not reproduce an existing character, logo, text, or watermark.

## Interface hierarchy

1. Panoramic illustrated home stage.
2. One featured intelligence item in the quiet side of the image.
3. A five-panel capability strip below the featured item.
4. Navigation and health controls at the edge of the composition.

All other views remain work-focused and readable, inheriting only the palette, material treatment, and typography.

## Interaction and accessibility

- Keep motion limited to one page entrance and direct hover/focus responses.
- Preserve keyboard focus, reduced-motion support, and readable contrast.
- On narrow screens, crop around the adviser without hiding navigation or featured intelligence.
- The home stage must remain usable when backdrop filtering is unavailable.

## Acceptance criteria

- The first viewport reads as one authored illustration, not a collection of cards.
- The female adviser is the only dominant decorative focal point.
- The left-side information remains readable without covering the character.
- The capability gallery is visible but clearly secondary.
- No remote image dependencies are introduced.
- Existing dashboard behavior and local-only server boundaries remain unchanged.
